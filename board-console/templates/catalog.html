Provider not listed? That means freehire has no adapter for it yet. To add a brand-new provider (e.g. a new ATS like InstaHire):

  1. Write and merge the Go adapter in internal/ingest/sources/
  2. Rebuild the stack: make up --build
  3. Add one row for it to board-console/data/combined_boards.csv (provider, board, company columns)
  4. Optionally add it to providerKind in providerkind.go, or it'll show under the "Unclassified" tab

It'll then appear in this dropdown.
